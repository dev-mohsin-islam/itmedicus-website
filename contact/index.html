Before you write

Support questions we answer most.

Four questions that come up often. Anything else, use the form above or email us directly — a person reads every message.

How do I get support for one of your apps?
Email us with the product name, the platform (Android, iOS, Windows or web), the app version and a short description of the issue. Messages sent to the product support address are routed to the team responsible for that app.
Are the ITmedicus apps free?
The core features of DIMS, PDM and Patient Aid are free to use. Those three apps also offer an optional premium plan that adds clinical content, guidelines, laboratory reference values or daily data updates. Premium is never required to use the basic reference.
Do you work with clients outside Bangladesh?
Yes. We are based in Dhaka and provide on-site support in Bangladesh, but software development, maintenance and support are delivered remotely to clients worldwide.
Can you maintain software that another company built?
Yes, under an agreed maintenance contract. We start with a review of the codebase, hosting and dependencies so the scope and the support level are understood before work begins.

Common questions

Before you get in touch.

A few questions we are asked most often. If yours is not here, send it — we answer it directly rather than pointing you at a brochure.

Ask a question
Is ITmedicus a software company or a product company?
Both. ITmedicus develops its own healthcare applications — such as DIMS, Patient Aid and PDM — and also builds custom software and digital platforms for healthcare and pharmaceutical organisations.
How do we start a custom software project with ITmedicus?
Send us a short description of the problem, the users involved and any timeline constraints. We begin with a discovery discussion, then prepare scope, a proposal and a delivery plan before development starts.
Can an existing product be adapted for our organisation?
In many cases, yes. Adaptation depends on the product, the requested changes and the deployment model. Contact us with your requirement and we will tell you honestly whether an existing product, a configuration or a new build is the better route.
Where can I get support for an ITmedicus application?
Product support is handled by email at our published contact address. Please include the product name, platform, app version and a description of the issue so we can route it correctly.
Do your clinical products replace professional judgement?
No. Our reference applications are informational and educational tools. They do not replace professional judgement, clinical training or official prescribing information, and they should not be used as the sole basis for a clinical decision.
Which platforms do the products support?
Support varies by product and is shown on each product page. The portfolio spans Android, iOS, web and Windows, but a specific product may not be available on every platform. Check the product page or store listing before relying on a particular platform.

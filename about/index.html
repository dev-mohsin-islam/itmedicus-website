About ITmedicus

Technology with a healthcare purpose.

ITmedicus is a healthcare technology company focused on developing digital applications, software and technology-enabled services for healthcare professionals, pharmaceutical organisations and the wider community.

Our story

Since 2011, at the intersection of IT and healthcare.

Since 2011, ITmedicus has worked at the intersection of information technology and healthcare. Our work has evolved from healthcare information applications into a broader portfolio of mobile apps, professional software and digital solutions.

We continue to focus on practical technology that helps people access information, manage workflows and connect with digital healthcare services — for clinicians at the point of care, for organisations running operations, and for the public looking for clearer answers.

Mission and vision

Our mission

To create useful, accessible and dependable digital solutions that support better healthcare information, professional workflows and healthcare experiences.

Our vision

To be a trusted healthcare technology partner, recognised for meaningful digital products and software that contribute to a more connected healthcare ecosystem.

Our values

Five principles that decide how we build.

These are not posters on a wall — they are the tests we apply when a project has to choose between what is easiest and what is right.

01

Purpose before technology

We solve meaningful user and business problems, and we let the problem choose the technology rather than the other way round.

02

Trust and responsibility

Healthcare information and user privacy are handled with care, because the consequences of getting them wrong are not abstract.

03

Usability

Complex information and workflows become easier to navigate. If a professional has to be taught to use a reference tool, we have not finished.

04

Continuous improvement

We listen, learn and improve products over time. A launch is a checkpoint in the product’s life, not the end of the work.

05

Collaboration

We work closely with healthcare and business stakeholders, since the people closest to a workflow usually know where it hurts.

How we work

A delivery process built on evidence, not assumptions.

Every engagement follows the same five stages. The depth changes with the project; the sequence does not.

01

Understand

We start with the users, the context and the operational challenge — not with a feature list.

02

Define

Scope, priorities, success measures and delivery milestones are written down and agreed.

03

Design & build

Design and development proceed with regular stakeholder feedback rather than one distant review.

04

Test

Careful testing across the relevant devices, platforms and real workflows.

05

Support & improve

Deploy, support, measure and improve — with maintenance and iteration planned from the start.

What it means in practice

A partner, not a vendor.

We stay involved after launch, because healthcare software is judged by how it behaves in month six, not in the demo.

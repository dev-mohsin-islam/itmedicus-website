Careers

Build technology that matters.

At ITmedicus, we bring technology and healthcare together. We welcome people who care about building useful products, solving problems and learning continuously.

View open positions Send your CV

Working here

Small teams, real products, direct feedback.

Our teams work close to the products they build and the people who use them. That means fewer layers between a decision and its consequence, and much more direct feedback from clinicians, pharmacists and patients.

We look for people who are comfortable with the ambiguity of healthcare domain work: reading carefully, asking a second question, and preferring a correct slow answer over a confident wrong one.

Products you can point to

Applications used by healthcare professionals and the public, not internal tools with no audience.

Healthcare domain depth

You will learn how clinical and pharmaceutical work actually happens, and why the details matter.

Craft over churn

We would rather ship fewer things that hold up than a stream of features nobody asked for.

Room to grow

People move between products and services, and pick up skills the work requires.

Open positions

Current opportunities.

Every role listed here is active. If nothing is listed, there is no open vacancy — but a speculative application is always welcome.

No vacancies are published right now.

We only list genuinely active roles here, so this page stays empty until a position opens. If you would like to be considered for future work, send a CV and a short note about what you would like to build.

How to apply

Send a CV and a short note about the kind of work you want to do. Please state the role, your location and your availability. We read every application, and we reply when there is a real fit — even if that takes a little time.

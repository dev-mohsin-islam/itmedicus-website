Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information ITmedicus collects when you use this website or contact us, how that information is used, and the choices available to you.

Who we are

ITmedicus develops healthcare applications, professional software and digital solutions. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to ITmedicus. You can reach us at info@itmedicus.com.

Information we collect

We collect information in three ways:

  • Information you give us. When you complete the contact form or email us, we receive the name, email address, organisation, phone number, enquiry type and message you provide.
  • Information collected automatically. Our web server and any analytics service we use may record technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page and the pages you visit.
  • Information from our applications. Some of our applications require an account. In those cases the application’s own privacy notice describes the data collected, and that notice governs the application rather than this website policy.

How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and provide the information or support you request.
  • To operate, maintain and improve this website and our products.
  • To understand which pages and content are useful, in aggregate.
  • To meet legal, regulatory and contractual obligations.
  • To protect against misuse, fraud and automated abuse of our forms.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use the details you submit through this website for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Cookies and analytics

This website uses only the cookies and similar storage needed to function. If we add an analytics service, it will be configured to minimise the data collected, and this policy will be updated to describe it before it is enabled. Where consent is required by applicable law, we will ask for it before setting non-essential cookies.

Sharing information

We share information only with service providers who help us operate the website and our business — for example hosting, email and support providers — and only to the extent needed to provide those services. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

International transfers

Some service providers may store data outside Bangladesh. Where that happens, we take reasonable steps to ensure the information receives an appropriate level of protection.

Retention

We keep enquiry records for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and for a reasonable period afterwards for reference, unless a longer period is required by law.

Security

We use technical and organisational measures intended to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration or loss. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you, correct inaccurate information, delete information we no longer need, or object to certain processing. To make a request, email us. We may need to verify your identity before acting on it.

Children

This website is intended for organisations and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

Medical information

Content on this website and in our reference applications is informational and educational. It is not medical advice and does not create a clinician–patient relationship. Do not send confidential clinical or patient-identifiable information through the website contact form.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services change. The “last updated” date at the top of the page will change when we do.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@itmedicus.com, or by post to F: 8/B, H:31, Eastern Housing, Basherpul, Demra, Dhaka, Bangladesh.
